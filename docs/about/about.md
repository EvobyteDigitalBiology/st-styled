# About EVOBYTE

This section summarizes the [EVOBYTE About page](https://www.evo-byte.com/about).

EVOBYTE is a data science and digital biology company focused on turning scientific and business data into practical software solutions. Their work combines analytics, engineering, and applied AI to build tools that help teams automate workflows, gain insights faster, and make better decisions.

Alongside client projects, EVOBYTE develops products like **st_yled** to make modern data app development easier and more effective for the broader community.
